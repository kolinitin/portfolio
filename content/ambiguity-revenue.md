## The Problem

E-commerce search is famously complex. Users don't always know what they want, and when they do, they use vocabulary that doesn't match the catalog. The existing search experience was a fragmented collection of legacy patterns.

## The Solution

We embarked on a comprehensive redesign of the search architecture. 

*   **Modular Design:** Created a component-driven framework that allowed the UI to adapt based on the category (e.g., fashion vs electronics).
*   **Predictive Filtering:** Implemented dynamic filters that anticipated user needs based on query intent.

## The Outcome

The redesigned experience led to a significant increase in conversion rate, translating to over $1B in additional annualized revenue.
