## Platform

## Users

Design leaders, founders, hiring managers, and product teams at startups or high-growth tech companies seeking senior/principal design leadership, product strategy, design systems expertise, or independent design consulting.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio website for Nitin Koli (Principal Product Designer) that showcases 15+ years of experience designing at scale (250M+ users), scaling design teams (6 → 55 designers), and navigating complex product challenges (e.g. Dream11 regulatory pivots, Watch Along). The site aims to build credibility, present detailed case studies, and drive inquiries/consulting engagements.

## Positioning

Independent Principal Product Designer with deep experience scaling digital products to 250M+ users and leading 50+ person design orgs. Combines high-level product strategy, design system architecture, and AI-augmented prototyping.

## Operating Context

Evaluated asynchronously by busy executives, recruiters, and founders looking through case studies, resume/CV downloads, metric highlights, and design leadership philosophy.
